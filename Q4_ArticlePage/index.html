The Rise of Artificial Intelligence

By Yashna Yogi | Published on November 4, 2025

Artificial Intelligence, or AI, is transforming the way humans interact with technology. From smart assistants to self-driving cars, AI is becoming deeply integrated into everyday life.

The field of AI is not new — it dates back to the mid-20th century. However, recent advancements in machine learning and deep learning have accelerated its growth dramatically, leading to breakthroughs in automation and prediction systems.

“The real question is not whether machines think but whether men do.” — B.F. Skinner

Industries across the world are rapidly adopting AI to improve efficiency and customer experience. Healthcare uses AI to detect diseases early, while the automotive industry relies on it for intelligent driving systems.

In education, AI enables personalized learning paths for students, adapting the pace and style of teaching to individual needs. Meanwhile, in business, companies leverage AI to analyze massive datasets, unlocking patterns invisible to the human eye.

Despite its benefits, AI raises ethical and social challenges — such as bias in data and job displacement. Thus, the development of AI must always align with responsible innovation and human values.
